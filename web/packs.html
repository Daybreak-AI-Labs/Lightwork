Specialist packs · governed specialists

Specialists you switch on, not prompts you engineer.

Lightwork ships a library of specialist packs across finance, tax, legal, privacy, IT and GRC, operations and many industries. Each pack is one file that defines a specialist: a fixed job, the tools it may and may not use, a risk ceiling, the compartment it is sealed into and a step-by-step workflow. Suites group the packs by function or industry. Agents draft and recommend; a person signs off.

01 Packs & suites

One specialist per pack, under maker-checker discipline.

A pack is a single specialist with one fixed job; a suite groups the packs for one function or industry. Each pack drafts and recommends; a person reviews and commits.

01

A fixed job

Every specialist has one bounded persona and one task: a 13-week cash forecaster, a 1099 extractor, a citation checker. No open-ended prompt to engineer.

02

Least privilege

Each pack gets a tool allow-list, a deny-list that always wins, and a risk ceiling it cannot exceed, bound to every action.

03

A person signs off

Agents draft and recommend; they don't commit. Work routes to a reviewer who approves and releases it: maker-checker, built in.

The skills and lessons packs learn as they work are part of Cortex, Lightwork's governed memory: tenant-exact, every read audited, every learned change reviewable and reversible.

02 Flagship packs

Flagship suites, deep enough to stand on their own.

Finance

Office of the CFO

The finance organization as agents: controllership, FP&A, treasury, assurance and reporting. Agents never move money; a maker-checker gate sits on money movement, with amount-aware approval tiers, a segregation-of-duties linter and sanctions screening.

Tax · packs + pipeline

Tax preparation

A documents-to-draft workflow: classify and extract W-2, the 1099 family, K-1 and 1098 into a standardized workpaper, then a first-pass Form 1040 and state computation. Every number cites the document it came from. The preparer reviews and signs; it never files.

Legal

Legal & Privacy

Contracts, litigation support, privacy regimes (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPL, APPI, LGPD and US state laws), AI and emerging-tech counsel, antitrust and board governance, with a citation checker that flags any quote it cannot find in the cited source.

04 The quality gate

Every pack clears the same gate.

Before a pack ships, it clears the same pack quality gate as every other: a bounded persona, a tool allow-list, a deny-list and a risk ceiling. A pack with an empty allow-list or no risk ceiling fails.

Checked on every change

lightwork domains-lint --ci runs in CI on every change to the repository, so a pack that weakens its own envelope fails the build.

Invariant tests run across the whole library: no drafting pack can reach a state-mutating tool, a pack still in onboarding never acts autonomously, a spawned agent never exceeds its parent's grant, a compartment seal never reaches another compartment, and the hard-refusal floor cannot be removed.

05 Need one that isn't in the library?

When no pack fits, build one the same way.

The agent factory creates a new pack from a description of the job, through a short interview, or from a reviewed record of a person doing it (lightwork learn-demo). Both paths run the same spine as the built-in packs: the draft is clamped to a least-privilege envelope, a person approves it before it exists, and provisioning fills in only what the approved workflow already declares.

01
Describe or demonstrate
An interview about the job, or a reviewed record of a person doing it. Secrets are redacted at the door.
02
Clamp
The draft is held to the same least-privilege envelope as a built-in pack, and its persona is Shield-scanned.
03
Approve
Nothing activates until a person approves it; a demonstration is the evidence they review.
04
Provision
Missing skills and declared tools are filled in, consent-gated and off by default, never wider than the approved envelope.
05
Learn
Approval and provisioning gaps feed back into how the next pack is drafted. Envelopes never widen.
