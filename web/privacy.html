This website · at a glance

Last updated 2 November 2026

Cookies

This website sets no cookies: no tracking cookies, no advertising cookies, no analytics cookies. We verified this: the site returns no Set-Cookie headers. Because nothing non-essential is stored on your device, there is nothing to consent to, which is why you won't find a cookie banner here. If we ever introduce a cookie that isn't strictly necessary, we will ask for your consent first and document it on this page.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand aggregate traffic: page views, referrers, and rough geography. It is privacy-first by design: it sets no cookies, does not fingerprint your browser, and does not track you across other sites. We do not run Google Analytics or any advertising tool, and we do not build a profile of your visit.

Third-party services

The site is delivered by Cloudflare: Cloudflare Pages for hosting and transport security, plus Cloudflare's cookieless Web Analytics. To serve a page and count a visit, Cloudflare necessarily processes a connecting IP address; it sets no cookies. Everything else, fonts, images, styles, is served first-party from this domain, so your browser makes no requests to Google or any other third party.

Information you give us

This website has no forms and collects nothing you type. If you email us, we receive whatever you send and use it solely to respond to you. We don't sell it, rent it, or share it with anyone outside Daybreak Labs, and we keep it only as long as we need it to handle your message. Please don't send confidential, health, or other regulated data by email; if you need to share something sensitive, tell us and we'll set up a secure channel.

Links to the Lightwork repository, its issues and its Discussions take you to GitHub. Anything you post there, such as an issue, a discussion or a pull request, is public and is handled under GitHub's own privacy terms, not this page. Report security vulnerabilities privately, as described in the project's security policy.

The Lightwork software vs. this website

This policy covers this website only. Lightwork is open-source software that you install and run in your own environment, on your own machine, servers, cloud account or an air-gapped network. The data an installation processes is under your control and your configuration, is not collected through this website, and is not covered by this page.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you, and to object to its processing. To exercise any of these, email chris@daybreakailabs.com and we'll respond within the timeframe the law requires.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about privacy at Daybreak Labs, go to chris@daybreakailabs.com. We may update this page as the site evolves; the date at the top reflects the latest version.
