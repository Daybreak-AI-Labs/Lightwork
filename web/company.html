Daybreak Labs · the lab at a glance

01 The thesis

Agents will do enterprise work. The question is whose governance they run through.

Frontier labs build smarter models, and that race is commoditizing by the month. The hard part, and the durable one, is the workforce around the model: governance bound to every action, an audit trail that's tamper-evident and verifiable offline, service levels measured from real work, and improvement a person reviews before it ships. In regulated work an agent nobody can vouch for is an agent nobody is allowed to use.

01

Governance bound to every action

Every action checked for scope, policy, safety and budget, then written to a signed audit.

02

An audit trail that's tamper-evident

The Operating Record: signed, hash-chained, verifiable offline.

03

Service levels measured from real work

When a whole function moves onto the agents, its target service levels are measured against completed work, and exceptions go to a person.

04

Improvement under review

Learned skills and proposed changes wait for a person's approval, a changed specialist can run as a trial first, and every change can be rolled back.

02 Why open source

A governance layer you can't read is one more thing you have to trust.

We publish Lightwork under the Apache License 2.0 because the claims that matter in regulated work have to be checkable: what the gates enforce, what the audit log records, and where each control stops. Anyone can read the code, run the proof, and change it.

03 What we build

One runtime, Lightwork. Six ways to put it to work.

Everything below runs on the same governed spine: every action checked for scope, policy, safety and budget, then written to a signed audit.

01 · Move a function

Autonomous Shoring

Tooling for moving a whole business function onto governed agents, with a book of work, measured service levels and an exception desk.

How it works → 02 · Platform

Lightwork Platform

The governed runtime, with the Operating Record underneath. Self-hosted or air-gapped.

How it works → 03 · The company brain

Cortex

The governed, tenant-exact memory the agents read from and write to, with every read audited.

Inside Cortex → 04 · Specialist packs

Specialist packs

Specialists across finance, tax, legal, privacy, GRC, operations and more, each with a fixed job, least-privilege tools and a human who signs off.

See the packs → 05 · Govern any fleet

Fleet governance

Bring the agents you didn't build under the same record: they deposit into and recall from Cortex under the same gates and audit.

How it governs → 06 · Compliance work

Autonomous Compliance

Privacy, GRC, third-party and AI-governance work done by governed agents, with a person reviewing and committing each result.

How they fit. Autonomous Shoring moves a whole function. The platform runs the work, the specialists do it, Cortex remembers it, fleet governance brings outside agents under the same record, and Autonomous Compliance applies all of it to privacy, GRC and AI-governance work.
