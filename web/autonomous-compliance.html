Open source · compliance work, run by agents

Compliance that does the work, under a record an auditor can verify.

Autonomous Compliance is the privacy, GRC and AI-governance side of Lightwork: six suites of dashboard modules where governed agents do the work. They work a privacy request, propose assessment answers from evidence you already have and keep the AI inventory current. A second person approves the steps that matter, and every action lands in the signed audit log. It ships in every Lightwork install and runs wherever you run Lightwork.

01 Why agents

A workflow platform tracks the work. It doesn't do it.

Most compliance platforms were built for a team that does everything by hand: they give that team forms, workflows, reminders and a dashboard. The team is still the bottleneck, the evidence is still collected before the audit, and the AI register is something someone has to remember to update.

A workflow-based compliance platform

  • The team does the work. The platform records that it was done.
  • Evidence is collected by people, on a calendar, before the audit.
  • The AI register drifts the day after it's filled in.
  • One deployment model. The vendor's.
  • Closed source. You take the vendor's word for what it does.

Autonomous Compliance

  • Governed agents do the work. A person approves the steps that matter.
  • Evidence is generated as the work happens: signed, hash-chained, verifiable offline.
  • The AI inventory is filled by read-only connectors to your model registries and AI platforms.
  • Self-hosted or air-gapped. The same gates either way.
  • Open source. Read the code behind every line on this page.
02 The six suites

The compliance program, as an agentic workforce.

Each suite is a set of dashboard modules worked by governed agents with fixed jobs and least-privilege tools. They draft and recommend; a person approves the steps that change something. Items marked Preview work but are still being expanded; everything else is shipped.

Suite 01

AI Governance

The AI inventory, and the obligations that follow from it.

  • Governance agents that keep the AI inventory and its reviews current
  • Read-only inventory connectors for MLflow, Amazon SageMaker, Snowflake Cortex, Salesforce Agentforce, Hugging Face, Azure AI Foundry, Amazon Bedrock, Copilot Studio, Databricks Unity Catalog and Vertex AI
  • Review and remediation tasks tied to each AI system
  • EU AI Act readiness, AI literacy and Article 73 serious-incident clocks and drafts Preview
  • Evidence analysis of uploaded documents about your AI systems Preview
Suite 02

Privacy Automation

Rights requests worked, not just tracked.

  • A privacy workspace for assessments (PIA, DPIA, TIA, LIA, CCPA, US state privacy, Colorado ADMT and others), vendors, requests, breaches and consent
  • Rights orchestration: retrieval, deletion and verification tasks, with opt-in source adapters for Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Salesforce and ServiceNow, independent approval for destructive work, and four-eyes closure
  • Privacy-notice releases that are independently reviewed, versioned and reversible
  • California DROP requests, and a public rights-request form with email verification
  • Discovery of where personal data lives in your storage Preview
Suite 03

Consent & Preferences

Consent as a record, not a banner.

  • Consent receipts and a governed preference center
  • Browser opt-out signals such as Global Privacy Control, applied to the matching requests
  • Publishing your consent banner and preference center Preview
  • Cookie and tracker scans of your websites Preview
Suite 04

Tech Risk & Compliance

Evidence generated as the work happens.

  • Framework scoping with control libraries for SOC 2, ISO 27001, NIST CSF, NIST 800-53, CIS v8, PCI DSS, HIPAA, CMMC Level 2 and FedRAMP Moderate
  • Assessment answers proposed from evidence you already have, approved by a different person before they are filled in
  • A no-code GRC studio for objects, fields, forms, rules, evidence bindings and report views, reviewed and approved before it is published
  • Typed sync of findings, risk acceptances and AI incidents to ServiceNow IRM and Archer
  • Continuous evidence collection from your systems Preview
Suite 05

Third-Party Management

Vendor risk with the contract on record.

  • Vendor onboarding, review and offboarding, step by step
  • Vendor contracts and engagements kept as records with their own lifecycle
  • Executive PDF reports built from exact source records, with governed downloads
  • Monitoring vendors for risk signals Preview
Suite 06

Data Use Governance

Every use of data has a purpose and a recorded decision.

  • Purpose capture, and a policy compiler that turns data-use decisions into machine-readable policy
  • Applying those rules natively inside Snowflake and Databricks Preview
03 How it's built

Agents, not forms. The same governance as everything else Lightwork runs.

Autonomous Compliance is not a separate system with its own security model. It runs on the Lightwork runtime, so every module above inherits the runtime's controls.

01
Capability
With capability enforcement on, an agent's grant can only narrow from its parent's, never widen.
02
Policy
Deletion, publication and case closure wait for a second, independent person.
03
Shield
Inputs, every tool call and every answer are scanned for dangerous actions and prompt injection.
04
Budget
The same dollar, time, token and tool-call caps as any other run.
05
Audit
The evidence an auditor asks for is the record the work produced, signed and verifiable offline.
Switches
Most modules sit behind their own switch and start off. An administrator turns on the ones they need, in the dashboard or in config.toml.
Tenancy
Records are scoped to the exact workspace they belong to. Sensitive local stores are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM by default, and the key can be supplied from your own KMS or secrets manager.
Deployment
Self-hosted or air-gapped. On your own machine, your own servers or your own cloud account. lightwork airgap checks the configuration for outbound paths.
Human role
Agents draft and recommend. Destructive and publishing steps need a second person, and an agent cannot approve its own request.
Interoperability
Read from your registries and platforms; write findings, risk acceptances and AI incidents back to ServiceNow IRM and Archer; export signed records.

05 How to roll it out

Run it beside what you have. Switch over when the record proves it.

Keep your current process running while the agents do the same work alongside it, every action recorded. Decide to switch over on the evidence, one suite at a time.

01
Shadow
Turn on one suite and connect your registries and platforms read-only. The agents do the same work beside your current process, and every action is recorded.
02
Switch over
Suite by suite, on the evidence in the record, with the people who approve already in the loop.
03
Prove
Answer the next audit from the record, signed and verifiable offline.
