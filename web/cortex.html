Cortex · the company brain

Everything your company knows, in one governed mind.

Cortex is Lightwork's memory: one governed search across your documents, the skills the agents have learned, the lessons they have proved, the Operating Record of what was decided, and what enrolled outside agents have contributed. Every read is floored to one tenant and leaves an audit row. It runs wherever you run Lightwork, on infrastructure you control.

01 What it holds

Four kinds of knowing, one memory.

A chatbot forgets the conversation. A framework keeps a vector store. Cortex is what an organization actually knows, kept in a form an agent can use and an auditor can trace. Each source keeps its own store and its own gates; Cortex gives them one query, one citation format and one audit trail.

Knowledge

Documents and data

Your documents and data, retrieved with citations, so every answer says where it came from.

Skills

How the work gets done here

Reusable recipes the agents learned on your work. A proposed skill is used in later runs only after a person reviews and admits it.

Lessons

What went wrong, and the fix

Failures are mined into guidance, validated on held-in and held-out cases, promoted as a watched canary, and can be retired or rolled back.

The record

What was decided, and why

The Operating Record: goals, outcomes, approvals and who decided, threaded from the signed, hash-chained audit log, so Cortex can recall not just facts but decisions.

02 Who writes to it

Every agent. Every person. Under the same audit.

Cortex is not a side database for one feature. It is the shared memory of the agents and of the people who supervise them, searchable from the dashboard, the REST API and the command line.

Lightwork's own agents

Specialist packs

The skills and lessons Lightwork's specialists learn as they work, under review and rollback, are sources Cortex searches.

Agents you didn't build

Agentforce, Bedrock, Copilot Studio, in-house

Through fleet memory, enrolled outside agents deposit lessons and recall them: roster-gated, Shield-scanned, secret-redacted, tenant-bound, and tagged with the vendor and agent that wrote them.

People

Approvals and decisions

When a person approves or denies an action, the decision and who made it go into the Operating Record, and Cortex can recall it the next time the same case comes up.

03 How it's kept

Your company gets smarter. Nobody else gets your company.

Each tenant's memory improves its own agents, on its own data, inside its own boundary. Lightwork runs on infrastructure you control, so there is no vendor cloud for your memory to be pooled into.

Tenancy
Tenant-exact. Every read is floored to the active tenant, and the answer carries the tenant it was answered for. Nothing is pooled or aggregated across tenants. Lightwork's built-in usage and failure counters are local files; metrics and traces go only to a collector you configure.
Keys
Sensitive stores are sealed with AES-256-GCM by default. Per-tenant data keys and cloud KMS (AWS, Google Cloud, HashiCorp Vault or Azure Key Vault) let you bring your own keys; with per-tenant keys on, one tenant's key never opens another's data.
Reads
Every read is audited: who asked, which sources answered and with how many hits, which were refused and why, and a digest of the query, never the query text. An outside agent is checked by the Agent Trust Plane before any source is touched.
Deployment
Self-hosted or air-gapped, wherever you run Lightwork. [cortex] enable = false turns the whole surface off; each source keeps its own switch.
Learning
Lessons are validated on held-in and held-out cases before they ship, watched as canaries, and reversible. The evaluator that scores improvement is held to locked ground-truth anchors, so it can't grade its own homework.
Sharing
Exchange between Lightwork instances is opt-in and operator-run. Bundles are Ed25519-signed, an import is refused unless its key is one you configured as trusted, and only consolidated lesson text crosses: never raw data or conversations.
