Governed evidence workbench

AI evidence analysis

Extract bounded, criteria-pinned technical evidence and route the result to an independently authorized human reviewer.

Trust boundary

Evidence and authority boundary

  • Results are technical evidence aids, not legal advice, certification, or compliance conclusions.
  • Raw artifact bytes are accepted only as a bounded request body and are not persisted by the evidence store.
  • Extracted text is not persisted; ephemeral process-memory zeroization is not guaranteed.
  • Tenant, operator identity, retention policy, adapters, source authority, criteria authority, and reviewer authority are resolved by the server.
  • Every result stays pending until a distinct authorized human approves or rejects it.
  • Parser availability is deployment-specific; unsupported, unavailable, active, or unsafe content fails closed.
