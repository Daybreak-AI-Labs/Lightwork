AI evidence analysis

Trust boundary

Before you use the findings

  • Results are technical evidence aids, not legal advice, certification, or compliance conclusions.
  • Uploaded files are processed within size limits and are not saved in the evidence store.
  • Extracted text is not saved. Immediate erasure from processing memory is not guaranteed.
  • Lightwork verifies your workspace, permissions, document source, review criteria, and retention rules before processing.
  • Every result stays pending until a distinct authorized human approves or rejects it.
  • Supported file formats depend on this installation. Unavailable, unsupported, active, or unsafe content is rejected.
