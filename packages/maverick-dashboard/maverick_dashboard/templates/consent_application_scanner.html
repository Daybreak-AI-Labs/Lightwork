Consent and preference governance

Mobile and connected-app manifest analysis

Review application manifests for Android, Fire OS, iOS, tvOS, Roku, Tizen, and webOS. Findings include source references and require a separate person to review the application version and result.

Evidence boundary

Manifest evidence is not live-device proof

  • Raw manifest bytes and free-form permission descriptions remain transient.
  • SDK names are unreviewed candidates, never automatic tracker or legal classifications.
  • Binary APK AXML, executables, device behavior, IDFA state, and runtime traffic are not inspected.
  • Local analysis does not establish native-SDK, platform, accessibility, recovery, scale, or customer acceptance.
  • Authorization follows the active tenant: tenant viewers inspect, tenant administrators operate, and tenant auditors verify custody.
