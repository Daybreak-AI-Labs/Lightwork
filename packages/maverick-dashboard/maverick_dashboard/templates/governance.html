Governance Workspace

Plain-language workflows for legal, privacy, compliance, and HR: fundamental-rights assessments, technical-documentation checklists, conformity routes, AI-literacy tracking, and serious-incident clocks. Every entry is recorded under your name in the governed ledger. Authority filings are recorded here; Lightwork does not submit them to an authority. The GRC sync action can send records to your configured external system after explicit confirmation. Model risk assurance shares these records.
