Governance Agents

Closed, evidence-cited workflows for breach response, privacy risk, program analysis, knowledgebase governance, and third-party risk.

Decision support, not autonomous authority. Raw evidence, tenant selectors, adapters, credentials, and dispatch targets are never accepted from this page. Every result requires an enrolled human review. Execution is separately enabled, two-person approved, idempotent, auditable, and default-off.

Privacy risk

Program copilot

Knowledgebase governance

Citation, freshness, access-scope and conflicting-guidance review.

Third-party risk

Intake, inherent risk, questionnaire, critical-event and remediation review.
