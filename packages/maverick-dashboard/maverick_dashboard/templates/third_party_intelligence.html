2. Pseudonymous vendor and relationship graph

3. Screening, findings, review, and completion

A detector cannot disposition the same finding. Missing, stale, incomplete, unlicensed, mismatched, or unreviewed evidence fails closed.

4. Risk policy, evaluation, override, and rollback

Policy author and approver must be different authenticated humans. Overrides and rollbacks append immutable decisions; they do not rewrite earlier receipts.

5. Enhanced due diligence tasks, evidence, and decision

Approval and rejection require governed tasks and retained evidence; escalation remains available. The opener cannot approve their own case.

6. Monitor, change alert, and durable outbox

Claim, failure, retry, acknowledgement, cancellation, and dead-letter state are durable. Run resolves an opaque destination only inside the delivery worker and never returns the recipient; it can cause an external notification side effect.

7. Consent-bound risk exchange

8. Retention and integrity

Purge uses the authority clock and preserves the append-only governance audit. Integrity failure is returned as an unavailable capability rather than a trusted partial result.
