Decision boundary

Review evidence before adding answers

  • Only current, human-approved AI Evidence Analysis records can support a proposal.
  • Partial, missing, conflicting, or low-confidence evidence can never become an automatically applicable answer.
  • Approval selects exact eligible questions; it does not change the assessment.
  • Before adding answers, Lightwork checks that the evidence and assessment are unchanged. It fills only unanswered questions and never overwrites a human answer.
  • Evidence references and integrity checks do not establish legal conclusions, prove a provider's claims, confirm customer acceptance, or provide an independently stored audit record.
