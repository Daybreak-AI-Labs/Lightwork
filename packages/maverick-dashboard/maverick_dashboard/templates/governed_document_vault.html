Governed document vault

Store approved documents securely in your workspace and share them through short-lived, one-time downloads.

Trust boundary

Protect documents and access

  • Upload an approved file with a display name. Storage paths, external download links, and encryption keys are not accepted here.
  • Documents are encrypted before storage. Each saved version has a verification fingerprint to detect changes.
  • Downloads are limited to the authorized recipient, expire quickly, and can be used once. Download secrets are not retained.
  • Local custody readiness does not prove external object-store custody, backup restoration, malware scanning, or live customer-workflow acceptance.
