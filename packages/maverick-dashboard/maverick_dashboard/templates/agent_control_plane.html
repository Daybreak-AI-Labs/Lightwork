What a verb does — and what it does not prove

Suspend revokes a principal's capability; contain durably protects the named principal from this page, but does not claim a live-swarm seal or subtree revocation; and attenuate narrows an external agent's ceiling. Halt requests the hardened deployment halt and is reported as successful only after its GET status confirms the requested scope. Roster reads refuse completeness and return unavailable when required authorities cannot be read. Control-plane audit emission is fail-soft during ordinary audit outages; if a configured audit guarantee refuses a row, the control change may already have landed. Re-read enforced state and inspect the ledger before relying on either.
