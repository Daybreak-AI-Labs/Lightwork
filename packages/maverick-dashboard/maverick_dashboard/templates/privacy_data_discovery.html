Acceptance and completeness boundary. No live provider adapters or provider/customer acceptance is claimed. A completed report covers only the exact enrolled source and declared scan bounds; it never proves that personal data is absent.
  • Unsupported formats: unstructured discovery currently accepts JSON, JSON-LD, XML, CSV, HTML, and plain text. PDF, Office, images, archives, audio, and video are reported as unsupported rather than silently treated as complete.
  • Cooperative deadline: the coordinator checks time between provider items. An adapter blocked inside its own call can overrun until it yields or returns.
  • Callback outbox limitation: approved data-map/RoPA callbacks are synchronous and have no durable delivery outbox or automatic retry. The review remains durably approved after a callback failure; an operator must reconcile delivery.
