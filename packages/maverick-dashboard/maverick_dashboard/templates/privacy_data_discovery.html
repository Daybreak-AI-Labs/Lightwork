Acceptance and completeness boundary. Six stock adapter contracts now ship for AWS S3, Azure Blob, Google Cloud Storage, Snowflake metadata, ServiceNow CMDB and Microsoft Entra assets. A configured adapter is not evidence of live provider/customer acceptance. A completed report covers only the exact enrolled source and declared scan bounds; it never proves that personal data is absent.
  • Unsupported formats: unstructured discovery currently accepts JSON, JSON-LD, XML, CSV, HTML, and plain text. PDF, Office, images, archives, audio, and video are reported as unsupported rather than silently treated as complete.
  • Cooperative deadline: the coordinator checks time between provider items. An adapter blocked inside its own call can overrun until it yields or returns.
  • Remediation boundary: approved classifications enter a tenant-private durable retry outbox and optional digest-only data-map target. Provider remediation writes remain disabled until a separate tenant authority is enrolled.
  • Managed retry boundary: recurring reconciliation and retry stays off unless privacy_automation.data_discovery.remediation.scheduler.enable is explicitly enabled at the deployment and tenant gates. The scheduler operates only the local digest-only target; it never runs provider discovery or proves estate completeness.

8. Four-eyes approval

The approver must be a different authenticated principal. Only successful fresh approval can invoke the injected data-map/RoPA callback.

9. Deliver, reconcile, and maintain the governed data map

Managed worker status is API-derived. The Runtime status action reports the tenant opt-in, running state, bounded cycle counts, interrupted-cycle recovery, and process/cross-process exclusion without opening a tenant remediation store or provider session.

Manual and managed worker identities are tenant/principal-derived. Worker and reconciliation operations are digest-only and bounded; they do not perform provider writes or prove live-provider acceptance.
