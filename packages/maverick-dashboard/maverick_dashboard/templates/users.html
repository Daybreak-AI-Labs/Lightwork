    What each role can do

    {{ 'admin' | role_label }}

    Runs Lightwork. Manages people and roles, settings, provider keys and secrets, plus everything an operator and an auditor can do.

    {{ 'operator' | role_label }}

    Does the work. Starts and cancels tasks, approves or denies actions waiting for a decision, and uses tools. Can't change settings or manage people.

    {{ 'auditor' | role_label }}

    Reviews the record. Reads the history of what happened and reports, and nothing else, so reviews stay independent.

    {{ 'viewer' | role_label }}

    Looks but doesn't touch. Sees dashboards and results.

    Custom roles

    Need something in between, say a compliance reviewer who both reviews and runs work? Make your own role. Every role can view; tick what it adds. Custom roles appear in every role list and in Settings. The four standard roles can't be changed or deleted.
