What can't be changed here

Sign-in and single sign-on, how workspaces are kept apart, encryption keys, where Lightwork stores its files, and payment service keys are set by whoever runs your server. They protect this page itself, so they can only be changed there. A setting marked “Set by your server's deployment settings” is fixed in the same way.

Where Lightwork keeps its files on the server, the file of sanctions names, and the server passwords that outside agents and the AI gateway use are also set there.

AI service keys, models, the spending cap per task, features, abilities, connections, plugins and the sign-in details of the six chat apps on the Channels page have their own sections on the Settings page and their own pages. The security suite's on and off switches are on the Security page.
