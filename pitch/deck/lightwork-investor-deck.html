01
The thesis

The agent runtime is commoditizing. The governance & proof layer around it is the ownable business.

Frontier labs make agents smarter every month — that race is a treadmill. The durable company is built on what sits around the agent: governance bound to every action, an audit trail that can't be forged, and a workforce that improves under proof. In regulated industries that layer is the difference between a demo and a deployment — and no one owns it.

02
The problem

Every regulated enterprise wants an agent workforce. Almost none can deploy one.

The blocker isn't capability — it's three questions no one can answer. So the pilot dies the moment the CISO, the compliance officer, and the GC walk in.

audit

“What did it do?”

No tamper-evident record of the actions an agent took.

security

“Could it exfiltrate our data?”

A hosted black box, or a framework the customer has to secure themselves.

drift

“Will it get worse?”

No proof it's improving instead of quietly degrading.

Today's options force a bad trade: hosted black boxes you can't run on your data, or ungoverned frameworks you have to secure yourself.
