01 Why replace the platform

A workflow platform tracks the work. It doesn't do it.

The compliance platforms of the last decade were built for a team that does everything by hand: they give that team forms, workflows, reminders and a dashboard. The team is still the bottleneck, the evidence is still collected before the audit, and the AI governance module is a register someone has to remember to update.

A workflow-based compliance platform

  • The team does the work. The platform records that it was done.
  • Evidence is collected by people, on a calendar, before the audit.
  • AI governance is a register that drifts the day after it's filled in.
  • One deployment model. Theirs.
  • Priced per module, per seat. The more you govern, the more you pay.

Autonomous Compliance

  • Governed agents do the work. A credentialed human reviews and commits.
  • Evidence is generated as the work happens: signed, hash-chained, verifiable offline.
  • The AI inventory maintains itself from your model registries, data platforms and agent fleets.
  • Self-hosted, air-gapped, or hosted by us. Your choice, same gates.
  • One workforce, six suites. Every agent under the same five gates and the same record.
02 The six suites

The whole platform, as an agentic workforce.

Each suite is a department of governed agents with fixed jobs, least-privilege tools and a risk ceiling. They draft and recommend; a person with the right credential commits. Nothing state-changing happens without passing the gate.

Suite 01

AI Governance

The inventory that keeps itself current, and the obligations that follow from it.

  • AI-system inventory and risk tiering, fed by connectors to MLflow, SageMaker, Snowflake, Hugging Face and Agentforce, and by the agent fleets Lightwork already governs
  • EU AI Act, ISO 42001 and model-risk (SR 11-7) control mappings; serious-incident reporting under Article 73
  • Review and remediation task lifecycle for every AI entity; policy-pack changes with re-attestation
  • Scheduled red-team runs with leases, and a regulatory feed that turns new rules into tracked work
Suite 02

Privacy Automation

Rights requests fulfilled, not just tracked.

  • DSAR intake to delivery: retrieval, deletion and verification across Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Salesforce and ServiceNow, with encrypted target custody
  • Assessments (PIA, DPIA, CPPA ADMT) with agent-proposed answers under independent review
  • Notice publication with double preview, versioned release and verified rollback; California DROP
  • Encrypted, tenant-private document custody for attachments and evidence
Suite 03

Consent & Preferences

Consent as a record, not a banner.

  • Consent records with bulk subject actions, custom indexes and bulk export
  • Web publish settings and application-level Global Privacy Control opt-out
  • A branded Trust Center on your own domain, with reviewed, encrypted brand assets
Suite 04

Tech Risk & Compliance

Evidence generated as the work happens.

  • Control frameworks (SOC 2, ISO 27001, DORA, HIPAA and a FedRAMP path) with governed evidence collection and analysis
  • A no-code GRC studio: objects, fields, forms, rules, collector bindings, report views and runtime projections
  • Outbound sync to ServiceNow and Archer, so the GRC system of record stays the system of record
Suite 05

Third-Party Management

Vendor risk with a first-class contract lifecycle.

  • Vendor inventory and questionnaires with agent-proposed answers, independent review and controlled application
  • Contract and engagement lifecycle as first-class objects
  • Role-aware executive PDF reporting over exact source manifests, with governed downloads
Suite 06

Data Use Governance

Every use of data has a purpose and a recorded decision.

  • Data inventory and classification, purpose policies, and a decision recorded for each use
  • One normalized read model across all six suites, with explicit completeness and provenance for every entity

Agents, not forms. The same five gates as everything else Lightwork runs.

Autonomous Compliance is not a separate product with its own security model. It is a set of governed specialist packs on the Lightwork runtime, which means every capability above inherits the platform's controls without exception.
