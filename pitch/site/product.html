06 Provable learning

A workforce that proves it improved.

A closed, audited loop, off by default and reversible by snapshot. Every proposed change is validated for real, causal improvement before it is promoted, graded on real outcomes once it ships, and rolled back automatically if the lift doesn't hold. No change touches behavior on a hunch. What survives becomes a lesson in Cortex, where the next agent to face the same case finds it.

proven

Proven before it ships

Every proposed change is tested for real, causal lift on work it hasn't seen before it is allowed to change behavior. Nothing ships on a hunch.

watched

Watched after it ships

A change ships watched and graded against real outcomes. It earns its place from wins, or it is pulled automatically when it stops paying its way. Failures win ties.

reversible

Reversible, and it can't grade itself

The whole loop is off by default and reversible by snapshot, and the judge that scores improvement is held to a locked benchmark, so self-improvement can never grade its own homework.

The workforce's own pass-vs-fail runs, held in Cortex, are what a change is judged against, and a release can't ship unless the improvement survives a placebo control and clears the release gate in CI. The full learning architecture is covered in a technical deep-dive we share with design partners under NDA.
