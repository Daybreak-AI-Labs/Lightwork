02 The Agent Factory

You describe the business. Lightwork synthesizes the department.

The Agent Factory takes a description of your operation, or a demonstration of the work itself, and produces a validated, custom department pack. Describe the job, or let it watch a person do it (observed actions and narration, secrets redacted at the door) and synthesize the agent through the same governed intake. It ships fail-closed, safe by default, every capability denied, then opens rights deliberately, one scoped grant at a time.

Held to the standard, not exempt from it

A Forge pack inherits the same governance, least privilege, and domains-lint quality gate as every specialist in the library: the same gate every built-in pack clears. Custom work is held to the standard, not exempt from it.

And the factory improves itself. It mines every provisioning and approval gap from real runs back into how it builds the next pack, so each department is better-scoped than the last, never wider, with a human review gate always appended.
