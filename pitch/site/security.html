01 The controls

Mapped, not promised.

What a security reviewer requires, and the mechanism in the platform that satisfies it.

What your reviewers requireThe mechanism
Your data stays inside your boundarySelf-host or air-gap in your VPC or fully offline, or run on Daybreak-hosted infrastructure with tenant isolation and your own keys (KMS / BYOK).
Egress locked by policyEgress lock (Enterprise mode): outbound traffic is allow-listed per tenant, model calls and HTTP tools alike, so a successful prompt injection still can't move data out that way. Pair with a network egress firewall to also cover raw-shell paths.
Least privilege, per actionCapability tokens (attenuate-only): an agent narrows its rights, never widens.
Separation of dutiesDual control: the person who initiates an action can never be the one who approves it. N-of-M sign-off, enforced.
Bounded blast radiusSandbox, network-off by default · hard budget caps on tokens, dollars, wall-clock, and tools.
A human stays in controlPolicy on every action: allow, deny, or require a human.
A provable, tamper-evident recordSigned, hash-chained audit (the Operating Record): tamper-evident, verifiable offline.
Evidence on demandProof Pack: an Ed25519-signed bundle that verifies offline and refuses to fabricate.
Provable safety posture in CIlightwork safety --json: shield, sandbox isolation, and egress policy as a machine-readable assertion your pipeline can gate on.
Your data trains no one's modelTenant-exact, never pooled: your Cortex and your record are encrypted with your own keys and are never read by, trained on, or aggregated with another customer's. No hivemind, no telemetry, no pooled data.

03 Deployment

Runs where your policy requires.

Self-hosted, air-gapped, or hosted by Daybreak Labs: log in and go. The same gates, the same record, and your own keys, whichever you choose.

01

Self-hosted

Your VPC: your cloud account, your keys, on the stack you already run. Nothing routes through us.

02

Air-gapped

Fully offline, with a one-command readiness check. The platform never has to reach the network.

03

Hosted by Daybreak Labs

Log in and go. Tenant isolation, your own keys via KMS / BYOK, and the same gates and the same record as the other two.

Self-hosted and air-gapped deployments run on Docker, Kubernetes, or a single VPS. Every option integrates with Salesforce and ServiceNow over the MCP server.

04 Audit & evidence

Proof a reviewer can verify, offline.

The Operating Record is a signed, hash-chained log of every governed action. Each entry signs the one before it. Alter a single row after the fact and the chain breaks, so tampering is detected, not assumed away.
