01 What it holds

Four kinds of knowing, one memory.

A chatbot forgets the conversation. A framework keeps a vector store. Cortex is what a company actually knows, kept in a form an agent can use and an auditor can trace.

Knowledge

Documents, data and primary sources

Your documents, systems of record and read-only public primary sources, retrieved with citations, so every answer says where it came from.

Skills

How the work gets done here

Reusable recipes the workforce learned on your operation: the way your close runs, the way your intake is triaged, the exceptions that always come up.

Lessons

What went wrong, and the fix

Failures are mined into guidance, proven to lift results on held-out work, shipped as a watched canary, and retired if the lift disappears.

The record

Everything that happened

The Operating Record: every action, decision and sign-off, signed and hash-chained, so Cortex can recall not just facts but what the company decided and why.

02 Who writes to it

Every agent. Every person. Under the same audit.

Cortex is not a side database for one product. It is the shared memory of the whole workforce, and of the people who supervise it.

Lightwork's own departments

Second Shift and Forge packs

Every specialist reads from Cortex before it acts and writes back what it learned, with the entry signed into the Operating Record.

Agents you didn't build

Agentforce, Copilot, in-house, open-source

Through Fleet Governance, outside agents deposit lessons and recall them: roster-gated, Shield-scanned, tenant-bound, with value attributed per vendor.

People

Approvals, corrections, decisions

When a human approves, denies or corrects, that decision becomes part of what the company knows, and the next agent to face the same case finds it.

03 How it's kept

Your company gets smarter. Nobody else gets your company.

The data and learning principle is non-negotiable: each customer's usage improves their own isolated workforce, on their own data, inside their own boundary.

Tenancy
Tenant-exact. One tenant's Cortex is never read by, trained on, or aggregated with another's. No hivemind, no telemetry, no pooled data.
Keys
Encrypted at rest with your own keys (KMS / BYOK). One tenant's key can never open another's.
Reads
Every read is audited and roster-gated. Recall is an action that passes the same five gates as anything else.
Deployment
Self-hosted, air-gapped, or hosted by Daybreak Labs. The same Cortex, the same gates, the same record; you choose where it lives.
Learning
Lessons are proven on held-out work before they ship, watched as canaries, and reversible. The evaluator itself only improves against a checksum-locked anchor.
Sharing
Cross-instance exchange is opt-in, operator-run and signed, and carries consolidated lessons only: never raw data, never automatic.
