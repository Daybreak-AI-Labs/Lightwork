An AI lab for agentic workforces you can prove.

Daybreak Labs is an AI lab that specializes in agentic workforces, built on an unusually strong foundation of compliance and governance. The point is simple: the people who run regulated operations can trust what the agents do, prove it to an auditor, and therefore actually put them to work. Lightwork is the product that carries all of it.

Book a demo How it works
The thesis

Agents will do enterprise work. The question is whose governance they run through.

Frontier labs build smarter models. That race is commoditizing by the month. The hard part, and the durable one, is the workforce around the model: governance bound to every action, an audit trail that can't be forged, service levels the runtime enforces, and a workforce that improves under proof. Trust is not a feature we added; it is the foundation everything else is built on, because in regulated work an agent nobody can vouch for is an agent nobody is allowed to use.
