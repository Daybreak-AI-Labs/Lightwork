What you won't find anywhere else

It rewrites its own harness.

It mines its own failure traces into one minimal guidance line per weakness, regression-tests it on a held-out corpus, and only then promotes it — consolidating LLM-free, so it can't be prompt-steered.

It can't silently get worse.

Each learned change ships as a watched canary, graded on real outcomes and pulled automatically if it stops paying off. A hindsight engine catches regressions before they ship; every change is signed and one command from rollback.

It grounds itself in primary sources.

Analyst packs are auto-granted 37 read-only public-data connectors — SEC EDGAR, FRED, Treasury, CourtListener, openFDA, PubMed — so work cites an authoritative source, not model memory.

Your other agents answer to it.

One signed memory-and-oversight plane that Agentforce, Copilot, in-house, and open-source agents all deposit into, recall from, and are audited against — with value broken out per vendor.
