01 Packs & suites

A turnkey department, under maker-checker discipline.

A pack is a single specialist agent with one fixed job; a suite is the department that bundles them. Each pack drafts and recommends; a credentialed human reviews and commits. Every suite is ready to switch on.

01

A fixed job

Every agent has one bounded persona and one task: a 13-week cash forecaster, a 1099 extractor, a citation verifier. No open-ended prompt to engineer.

02

Least privilege

Each agent gets a least-privilege tool set and a risk ceiling it cannot exceed: a narrow allow-list, an explicit deny-list, bound to every action.

03

A human signs off

Agents draft and recommend; they don't commit. Work routes to a credentialed reviewer who certifies and releases it, maker-checker, built in.

Every pack reads from and writes to Cortex, the company brain: the governed, tenant-exact memory of what your company knows, the skills it has learned, the lessons it has proved, and the Operating Record, every read and write under audit, encrypted with your keys, never pooled across customers.

02 Flagship packs

Flagship departments, deep enough to stand on their own.

Finance

Office of the CFO

The finance org as agents: controllership, FP&A, treasury, assurance, and reporting. A hard “never move money without a human” guardrail, amount-aware approval tiers, a segregation-of-duties linter, and OFAC/SDN sanctions screening.

Tax · packs + pipeline

Tax for CPA firms

A documents-to-draft workflow: classify and extract W-2, the 1099 family, K-1, and 1098 into a standardized workpaper, then a first-pass 1040 plus the resident-state return. Every line cites its source document. It never files.

Legal

Legal & Privacy

Contracts, litigation support, privacy regimes (GDPR/CCPA/PIPL/APPI plus US state laws), AI and emerging-tech counsel, antitrust, and board governance, with citation verification that strips any authority it can't confirm.
