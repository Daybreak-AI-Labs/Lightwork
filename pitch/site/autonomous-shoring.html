01 The lineage

Every shoring decision traded control for cost. This one gives both back.

Offshoring cut cost and lost control of the work; that loss is why reshoring happened. Autonomous Shoring keeps the work inside your perimeter and binds governance to every action it takes, so the cost case and the control case point the same way for the first time.

02

03
Reshore
Control and data back home, at domestic labor cost.

Never left. The cost you are trying to move.

02 What you get

Everything a BPO contract has. Plus the record it never could.

A shoring engagement is a function, not a task. Each piece below maps to a mechanism in Lightwork, not a promise on a slide.

01

A book of work

Which function moves, step by step, and which steps stay human. Drawn from 2,020 governed specialist packs across 53 suites, so the starting point is a department, not a blank prompt.

02

A transition, not a prompt

The knowledge transfer a BPO takes months to run: watch a person do the job, and the agent factory synthesizes the governed pack that does it, clamped to a least-privilege envelope and approved by a human before it runs.

03

Service levels you can enforce

Hard caps on dollars, wall-clock, and tool calls on every unit of work, and throughput reported per department. A cap is a mechanism the runtime enforces, not a clause you hope for.

04

An exception desk

Maker-checker, built in. Agents draft and recommend; anything above the risk ceiling routes to a credentialed human who signs off. You always know where the humans still sit.

05

The Operating Record

Every action signed and hash-chained, verifiable offline, exportable as a sealed capsule. What a BPO's sampled QA could never give you: the whole function, every decision, tamper-evident.

06

A Shoring P&L

The number a CFO asks for first, from real completed work: agent cost against your own fully-loaded human rate per deliverable, throughput per department, and the improvement curve over time. Descriptive, never used to defund safety.

03 Autonomous BPO

The functions we shore first.

The work BPOs are hired for, run by governed agents with a human on the exceptions. Each of these ships as a standalone concierge today or as a department of packs on the platform.

Privacy operations
Data-subject requests end to end: intake, identity verification, the statutory clock, and fulfillment packages assembled only from your system extracts. Erasure is prepared as an operator handoff, never destructive on its own.
Governance & compliance evidence
Control evidence, vendor risk, policy attestations, and audit-ready packages (SOC 2, EU AI Act, model risk) generated as the work happens, with every agent action mapped to a control.
Finance operations
Reconciliation, vendor and payment controls, close support, and reporting from the Office-of-the-CFO packs, under a hard rule that no agent moves money without a human.
Model risk & AI assurance
An assurance officer for the models and agents you already run: inventory, risk classification, testing evidence, and the paper trail a second line of defense expects.
Legal & contract operations
Intake, first-pass review, and drafting across contracts, privacy regimes, and board governance, with citation verification that strips any authority it cannot confirm.
Security operations
Threat-hunting triage across your platform and environment, with findings staged for an analyst rather than actions taken on their own.

05 How an engagement runs

Scope. Demonstrate. Approve. Run. Prove.

The same five steps for every function, so the second one is faster than the first.

01
Scope
Pick one function. Split its steps into agent work and human decisions. Set the caps.
02
Demonstrate
A person does the job once, narrated. The factory synthesizes the pack from the demonstration.
03
Approve
The pack is clamped to a least-privilege envelope, linted, and signed off by your reviewer.
04
Run
Shadow, then assisted, then autonomous. Exceptions route to the desk. Every action is recorded.
05
Prove
Each period closes with the Operating Record and the Shoring P&L, ready for finance and audit.
