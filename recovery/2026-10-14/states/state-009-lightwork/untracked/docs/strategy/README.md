# Strategy-document claim boundary

> **Strategy status - source-only / historical / proposal:** This directory is
> a set of dated internal planning artifacts. Checked-in paths and capability
> descriptions are source observations only; past measurements, market
> comparisons, and review outcomes are historical only; targets, timelines,
> valuations, positioning, demos, and future states are proposals only.

None of these documents is evidence of a supported release, hosted test result,
production qualification, customer outcome, return on investment, valuation,
compatibility, certification, legal conclusion, service level, or current
market position. Exact product claims must be re-verified against the immutable
candidate revision and the claim/evidence ledger. External market facts require
fresh dated primary sources. Customer, operating, security-assessment, and
financial claims require independent evidence from the named environment.

Dynamic repository totals do not belong in strategy prose. In particular, do
not quote aggregate test, pack, suite, connector, route, skill, or agent counts
from this directory. Generate an exact-revision inventory through the release
process instead, and keep local or historical test execution separate from a
hosted or release-qualified result.

Forward-looking material has no approval or commitment effect. A named human
owner must approve any external use and replace assumptions with current,
sourced facts before publication.
