# Program documents are roadmap proposals

> **Roadmap/proposal only:** every document in this directory is a planning kit
> for a possible future program. None establishes an operating program, current
> offer, partnership, certification, event, grant, sponsorship, license,
> published asset, supported product capability, or customer result. Lightwork
> source `0.1.7` is unreleased and has no supported production release.

These documents may contain draft emails, event scripts, award criteria,
certification language, budgets, or descriptions of broader source modules.
They are reusable planning inputs, not externally approved statements. Before
using any part of a kit outside the repository, a named owner must:

1. verify every product, integration, count, command, and compatibility claim
   against the current source-derived inventory and production support matrix;
2. replace proposed dates, money, staffing, legal terms, and service levels
   with approved values;
3. remove claims for surfaces excluded from the exact release;
4. obtain legal, security, brand, and release approval as applicable; and
5. retain the approved material and supporting evidence with the change record.

Words such as “certified,” “validated,” “shipped,” or “production” inside a
template describe proposed criteria or copy. They do not confer status. No
program document may be used as release, certification, customer, performance,
ROI, compatibility, or compliance evidence.
