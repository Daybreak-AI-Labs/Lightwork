# Commercial agreement checklist

> **Draft checklist only; not a license grant, EULA, offer, or legal advice.**
> The root proprietary `LICENSE` permits use only under separate written
> permission. Counsel must draft and approve the actual agreement.

A written evaluation or commercial agreement should resolve, as applicable:

- parties, authority, effective date, order of precedence, scope, term, renewal,
  termination, and post-termination transition;
- exact licensed release/surfaces, users, tenants, environments, restrictions,
  evaluation limits, and audit/verification rights;
- fees, taxes, invoicing, expenses, suspension, and change-order mechanics;
- ownership, license grants, feedback, customer materials, generated output,
  configuration, custom work, trademarks, and third-party components;
- warranty, disclaimer, indemnity, liability, insurance, and dispute terms;
- confidentiality, security schedule, DPA/privacy roles, subprocessors,
  transfers, incident notice, retention/deletion/export, and legal holds;
- support scope, hours, severity, targets, maintenance, deprecation, upgrades,
  dependencies, customer responsibilities, and service credits if any;
- acceptable/prohibited use, high-risk or regulated use, human oversight,
  model/provider terms, and suspension or emergency containment;
- accessibility commitments, documentation, training, and procurement evidence;
  and
- governing law, notices, assignment, force majeure, publicity, compliance,
  records, survival, and entire-agreement terms.

Attach only exact-release claims supported by the approved claim-evidence
ledger. Source inventory, planned features, blank templates, local tests, and
unqualified external dependencies must not become contractual promises by
reference.
