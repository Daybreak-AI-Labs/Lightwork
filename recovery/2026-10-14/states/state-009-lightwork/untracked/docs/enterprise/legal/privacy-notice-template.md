# Privacy notice template

> **Draft template only; not legal advice or a current Lightwork notice.** The
> deploying organization and counsel must complete this for the exact service,
> roles, jurisdictions, data map, subprocessors, and release. Delete all
> instructional text before publication.

**Notice owner:** `<legal entity and contact>`  
**Effective / last reviewed:** `<dates>`  
**Applies to:** `<named service, deployment, users, and jurisdictions>`

## 1. Roles and scope

State who determines processing purposes and means, who processes on another
party's instructions, and which activities are outside this notice. Do not
infer controller/processor roles from the software's self-hosted topology.

## 2. Data collected

Complete a table from the approved data map. At minimum consider identity and
authorization attributes, prompts and responses, tool inputs/outputs, goal and
flow state, audit/security events, model-provider traffic, usage/billing data,
support evidence, attachments or knowledge stores if enabled, and device or
network metadata. Remove categories the exact release refuses or does not use.

## 3. Purposes and legal bases

For each category, name the specific purpose, legal basis or other authorized
ground, whether provision is required, and the consequence of not providing
it. Use counsel-approved language; repository configuration is not a legal
basis.

## 4. Recipients, subprocessors, and transfers

Link the completed subprocessor register. Name every model, hosting, identity,
telemetry, messaging, storage, and support recipient, its purpose, region, and
transfer mechanism. Do not state “no egress” without exact network and provider
evidence.

## 5. Retention, deletion, export, and legal holds

Publish approved periods by data category, the event that starts each period,
backup/log handling, legal-hold precedence, export method, deletion method,
verification evidence, and residual limitations. Source commands do not prove
that external stores, provider copies, managed backups, or every optional
surface were covered.

## 6. Automated processing and human review

Describe the actual use of models or agents, meaningful human review, possible
effects, contest/escalation path, and any decision category the deployment
permits. Remove this section only on counsel's advice; do not equate an approval
UI with a legal conclusion.

## 7. Individual rights and requests

Name available rights for the applicable jurisdiction, identity-verification
process, request channels, response timing, authorized-agent process, appeals
or complaints, and regulator contact where required. Do not promise complete
export or erasure until the exact data map and live exercise support it.

## 8. Security, incidents, and changes

Describe approved safeguards at an appropriate level, the incident contact and
notification process, notice-change process, and effective date. Avoid
certification, “tamper-proof,” “zero risk,” or absolute-security language.
