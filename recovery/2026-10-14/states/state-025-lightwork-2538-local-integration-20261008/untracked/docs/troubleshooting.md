# Troubleshooting Lightwork

Use this guide when a page, task, or workflow does not behave as you expect.
Start with the current status shown in Lightwork; an empty list or a saved
draft does not mean work has finished.

## A task will not start

Open **Get started** and read the first incomplete setup step. If Lightwork
asks you to connect an AI service, an administrator can open **Settings** and
finish the connection there. If the service is connected but unavailable,
review the selected model and its current connection status before trying
again.

If the task is over a spending or time limit, review the amount shown before
starting. Ask an administrator to change a workspace limit only when the
business need and the available budget are clear. A task that was refused
before starting has not used a provider to do the requested work.

If a button is missing or unavailable, check that you are in the right
workspace, that the feature is turned on, and that your role includes the
needed access. Ask an administrator to review the access or setup message;
do not assume that changing your role is the right fix.

## A task is waiting or stopped

Open the task from **Tasks** or **Chat** and read its current status. When it
asks a question, answer in that task's conversation. Work cannot continue
until the answer is received.

For a paused, failed, or stopped task, read the saved reason and the next
available action. Use **Try again** or **Continue** only when Lightwork offers
it and the source information is still current. A new attempt may repeat some
work; stopping a task does not undo changes already made in another service.

If the task says it finished, open **Results** and review the saved answer.
Check important facts against the original request and source material before
sharing or acting on the result.

## A workflow does not run

Open the workflow and check that it is turned on and that you are using the
version you reviewed. Fill every required input. If a step uses another
service, open **Connections** to check the selected account and its current
status. A connection test confirms only that check at that time.

If the run pauses for approval, open the linked approval and review the exact
change. If a step failed or the service response is unclear, inspect the run
before retrying; an unclear result does not prove that the outside service
made no change.

## Copilot gives an answer that does not match the page

Ask Copilot a more specific question about the page or record you have open.
Its live answers are limited to information you are allowed to see. When it
offers a control, review the current details and confirm the action yourself.
Check the saved record afterward. A written answer by itself is not proof that
a setting, task, approval, or outside service changed.

## An approval is missing or needs a fresh review

Open **Approvals** or **Action Center** and refresh the list. Some decisions
require a different person from the one who prepared the change. If a review
says that its details changed, open the current record and review it again;
do not approve an older copy.

If a source is unavailable or a list says it may be incomplete, check the
linked record or return when that source is ready. An empty partial list does
not prove that nothing is waiting.

## You cannot find a result or older task

Open **Tasks** to check whether the work is still running, waiting, paused, or
failed. **Results** contains finished work and may have its own page controls
for older items. A saved draft appears in the workbench where it was created;
it is not automatically published or used in later work.

## You cannot see another person's work

Workspace membership does not automatically grant access to another person's
private task or conversation. If the work is needed for a business reason, use
the relevant access-review control and follow its approval process. Do not ask
someone to share a private result outside the review process.

## You need to report a problem

Open **Help** and prepare a support report. Review the exact text before you
copy or download it. Reports can contain workspace names or business details;
remove anything you do not intend to share. Lightwork does not send the report
automatically. Never include passwords, service keys, or another person's
private work.

When asking for help, include the page or task, what you expected, what the
screen showed, and the time it happened. Keep any failure reference shown by
Lightwork; it helps an administrator find the matching restricted log entry.

For the normal task and approval journeys, see [Use Lightwork](using-lightwork.md).
For unfamiliar product terms, see the [glossary](glossary.md).
