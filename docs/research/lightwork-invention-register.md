## How to retain and evaluate ideas

Keep each identifier stable. Add evidence and changes in disposition; retain failed variants and their original results. Merge duplicate descriptions by recording their relationship rather than claiming a second invention. A new implementation name does not reset an unfavorable experiment.

Every candidate needs an exact proposed mechanism, closest relevant prior art, a strong comparator with the same information and resources, a result that would defeat the claim, an accessible customer journey, and a reason the capability matters commercially. A passing implementation test alone cannot establish any superiority claim. Permission to use or export customer-derived information must be explicit.

Historical research notes remain intact. Their broad market, novelty, filing, and shipped-capability claims are historical statements, not renewed findings. Current source inspection and a bounded literature review cannot establish exhaustive novelty, patent clearance, or a purchase price.

## New research candidates

### N01 Preserve achievable customer commitments

**Hypothesis:** maintain the feasible continuations for all accepted customer obligations, then select observations, reversible reservations, and authorized actions before useful options disappear.

Illustration: two orders share production capacity. An expiring material reservation preserves one delivery route; a second supplier has unconfirmed stock. Approval delay can close both routes even though every action taken so far was individually permitted. The controller needs to recognize that loss of options before the final missed deadline.

The proposed technical object is a compact representation of feasible continuations across the modeled possible external states. Commitments contribute deadline, resource, quality, authority, and cost constraints. Shared resources connect commitments into interacting groups. Counterexamples refine the affected group and its assumptions. Observation, computation, reservation cost, and employee interruption all consume explicit budgets.

**Possible advance:** an effective factorization and online refinement algorithm for partially committed, asynchronous business work. Temporal commitments, viability control, robust scheduling, and model-predictive control are established. Cordon already supplies task-scoped semantic transactions. This proposal cannot claim those primitives as inventions.

**Test:** compare with an authored robust scheduler and controller, event-driven replanning, and durable workflows using identical observations and budgets. Hold out environments containing resource contention, expiring options, uncertain durations, approvals, delayed callbacks, and irreversible actions. Measure avoidable broken promises, conservatively rejected feasible work, throughput, reservation cost, and employee interventions together.

**Reject the invention claim if** a strong robust scheduler matches the result, gains arise from accepting fewer jobs or buying more capacity, or engineers must hand-author each feasibility boundary. Conditional feasibility under a declared uncertainty model cannot guarantee against arbitrary external changes.

October 2 research checkpoint: a bounded feasibility model assesses two manually entered accepted orders, reported capacity and stock, expiring conditional options, and owner-reviewed observed contention. A durable account/workspace-bound pilot now exposes ordinary forms, attributed source-review times, current assessments, owner decisions, recovery after uncertain responses, and typed Copilot controls behind an optional feature setting. It does not connect to a customer order system, independently verify stock or supplier claims, reserve or purchase anything, model full production precedence and quality, or qualify a customer deployment. The pilot caps the tenant's active review namespace at 1,000 records across its account contexts. Its owner can finish and archive a review in the page or Copilot, retaining its saved history before freeing an active slot. An independently implemented exhaustive robust scheduler matched all 27 small, fully observed comparator scenarios; these tests support correctness on their stated cases, not superiority or novelty. Wider held-out experiments and customer acceptance remain open.

Customer experience: “Friday remains achievable. Reserving these materials before 2 p.m. preserves both production options.” The person sees the affected commitments, supporting evidence, cost, authority, available options, and a real permitted decision.

### N02 Transfer executable lessons from private failures

**Hypothesis:** turn a particular private operational failure into an approved parameterized experiment that another installation can test against its own local operations.

Illustration: an approved revision changes between validation and commitment. The originating customer can permit release of that structural failure pattern while retaining actual records, identities, values, and procedures. A receiving installation generates a local scenario that tests whether its own approval mechanism has the same weakness.

The proposed synthesis algorithm must satisfy three connected obligations:

1. Reproduce the defect in a declared bounded source model and show the relevant modeled change removes that violation. One observed incident alone does not establish its real-world cause.
2. Establish that the exported artifact reveals no forbidden distinctions between source states with the same explicitly permitted release. Existence, timing, rare structure, branches, and cumulative releases matter as well as names and values. A pattern that is not permitted for release remains local.
3. Construct and check a target-specific mapping to local action and effect semantics. Return a locally reproduced violation, bounded non-applicability, or unknown. Do not treat similarity of wording as applicability.

A candidate method would search for a small parameterized witness while alternating failure-fidelity counterexamples and forbidden-disclosure counterexamples. A local receiver would synthesize a semantic mapping and produce its own checkable witness. This is a proposed method, not an implemented or established synthesis algorithm. Its declared model, language, bounds, permitted release, and local qualification must be explicit.

**Possible advance:** jointly automatic mechanism-preserving witness synthesis, conditional noninterference, and local semantic retargeting. Federated privacy-preserving test generation already exists. Sanitized crash reproducers, delta debugging, semantic mapping, and generic shared regression tests are mandatory comparators.

October 5 implementation checkpoint: the default-off Approval challenges page
and Copilot can generate a closed fictional approval-race program, reproduce
its failure in a deliberately weakened disposable copy, and check the same
schedule against this installation's native approval guard. Four declared
families cover a changed claim, changed request, reused review after undo, and
expiry, with one or two required sign-offs. The finite model uses one request,
two reviewers, up to 12 transitions and 50,000 states. The fixed local receiver
has a 10-second deadline and fresh configuration, keys and databases.

Sharing requires separate consent. The small imported file must exactly match
the supported closed language; it supplies no code, private incident, identity
or installation fingerprint. The receiver binds its result to supported
installed source bytes and runtime entry points. Users can review saved states
and remove inactive practice records with retained audited removal receipts.
This is an executable model-only pilot. It does not mine a customer failure,
learn a semantic adapter for another product, activate a production rule,
establish private-incident noninterference, or demonstrate novelty or superiority.

**Test:** independently authored failure families with varied schemas, connectors, rules, and event schedules; one observed source failure; receiving organizations and semantic variations withheld. Give every comparator the same permitted release and resources. Count extraction, model construction, synthesis, mapping, verification, and human review. Measure target defects correctly detected and localized, false accusations, legitimate work retained, and forbidden disclosure under adaptive probing.

**Reject the invention claim if** any of the three obligations fails, an engineer supplies the supposedly learned failure semantics, or sanitized reproduction plus ordinary local synthesis matches the result. Set numerical superiority thresholds before the comparison, after measuring baseline variance.

Customer experience: “A newly learned failure pattern may affect your approval process. Lightwork tested it locally and found a revision change could bypass the current check. Review the proposed correction.” An owner reviews exactly what would be shared; recipients inspect local applicability and can decline or reverse changes.

This differs from P15's fleet memory because the proposed reusable object is an executable challenge with explicit applicability and release obligations. It remains related to P08's fault-witness generation; adding export and retargeting does not automatically establish novelty.

### N03 Transport executable competence through local qualification

**Hypothesis:** discover a reusable abstract procedure together with organization-specific adapters and proof obligations. Keep local policy, thresholds, identities, and source records local.

The search would jointly optimize the abstract program, local adapters, and target qualification cost, subject to each adapter preserving the declared procedure semantics and invariants. A target installs locally verified behavior rather than inheriting the source customer's confidence or return-on-investment claims.

**Possible advance:** automatically discover a useful common program abstraction and sufficient target obligations across structurally different operations. This overlaps P01, P11, template synthesis, process transfer, formal skill containment, and proof-carrying actions. It has a weaker novelty case than N02.

**Test:** hold out businesses, procedure combinations, and model providers. Compare against shared templates plus a coding agent that synthesizes local adapters with the same verifier. Match all semantic annotation, model, verification, and human setup effort. Measure reliable coverage, qualification cost, and negative transfer.

**Reject the invention claim if** manual semantic mapping supplies the advantage or ordinary templates and local synthesis match it. Portable logical obligations cannot transport the originating customer's measured business benefit as a proven target benefit.

Customer experience: “This capability is ready to test against your approval process. These two local rules still need confirmation.” Adoption, limits, evidence, and rollback remain inside Lightwork.

### N04 Restructure operational work while preserving obligations

**Hypothesis:** discover when work can be split, merged, batched, moved, or reordered, then execute the changed organization while retaining each customer's obligations.

A bounded rewrite language proposes transformations. A verifier checks quantities, approval, confidentiality, deadlines, cost, and effects under relevant interleavings. A learner qualifies the conditions and cost of each transformation. Already released effects remain part of actual state; reversing future grouping decisions does not undo them.

**Possible advance:** learned conditional transformations of the work itself. Invariant-aware batching, coordination avoidance, scheduling, compiler optimization, and dynamic agent topology already cover much of this direction. N04 overlaps P02 and is a lower-priority novelty candidate.

**Test:** mixed workloads with genuine opportunities for shared work, urgent exceptions, confidential subsets, delayed approvals, and policy changes. Compare with authored invariant-aware batching and scheduling. Count repeated work, total cost, latency, integrity failures, and teaching effort.

**Reject the invention claim if** ordinary batching captures the gains or transformation qualification costs exceed their value.

Customer experience: “These jobs can share one preparation step. Three remain separate because of approval and deadline requirements.” Every original job retains its own status, rights, and evidence.

### N05 Share decision evidence across simultaneous business cases

**Hypothesis:** choose observations that resolve several pending decisions at once, while preserving case-specific constraints and evidence validity. For example, one verified supplier-capacity fact may resolve many order decisions but only within its recorded scope and freshness.

The proposed object is a shared decision-sufficiency certificate: within a bounded action and uncertainty model, the acquired observations establish a choice that remains acceptable across the unresolved states. One fact can support several cases only when meaning, authority scope, source, and freshness match. Expiry invalidates its dependent certificates. The technical opening would be an effective algorithm for learning and updating these shared dependencies under correlated facts, changing cases, and competing deadlines, with a global question and observation budget.

**Test:** independently specified queues with shared and private hidden facts, correlated answers, deadlines, and changing sources. Compare with per-case acquisition and a strong portfolio value-of-information method using the same global cache, joint inquiries, permissions, and loss constraints. Measure information cost, missed decision windows, verified decision quality, and employee interruptions. Generic value-of-information selection, caching, shared active sensing, and combinatorial elicitation are established.

**Disposition:** secondary research lead. No distinct mechanism or advantage established. Do not build a question dashboard and call it an invention. Reject the claim if strong globally informed acquisition and caching match it or the benefit is better prompting alone. This is related to P03 and P14 rather than an independent discovery merely because it spans more cases.

Customer experience: “One confirmation could unblock nine orders.” Show which work depends on the fact, who may obtain it, when it expires, and what remains unresolved.

### N06 Discover the operational capability that unlocks additional work

**Hypothesis:** learn which missing fact, permitted action, resource, or connector capability would make presently infeasible opportunities achievable, and produce an independently testable explanation of that boundary.

The proposed object is a capability-expansion frontier: independently testable minimal changes to the current feasible capabilities, together with the work they would make achievable. Maintain competing bounded capability models; search for additions that change reachability across those models; select approved tests to distinguish missing capability from missing evidence. Complementary capabilities need joint qualification because dates, resources, quantities, and rights can conflict.

The business question is specific: which affordable change would unlock which additional work without displacing existing commitments or violating the customer's limits? A learned explanation would need local evidence and an executable test. Permission, availability, and customer willingness cannot be inferred solely from an API schema.

**Test:** a bounded independently specified fulfillment environment with some capabilities, conflicts, and permissions hidden from the learner; sparse traces; held-out interventions checking proposed expansions. Compare with capability learning plus weighted planning and abduction, a strong general agent, and an information-matched known-model reference. Measure valid new work identified, false feasibility, minimality, qualification effort, and contribution under the environment's declared economics. Simulator economics are not evidence of customer revenue.

**Disposition:** secondary research lead, related to P12 and N01. Constraint relaxation, planning with new actions, and combinatorial opportunity construction are close precedents. No distinct algorithm or demonstrated benefit established. Reject the claim if ordinary abduction over the same learned model matches it or the proposed expansions rely on unverified capacity. A missing-capability label or a list of suggested purchases is insufficient.

Customer experience: “These additional offers could become feasible if this condition is confirmed.” Show the evidence, qualification step, cost, and capacity consumed. Approved qualifications may create named usable capabilities; proposed possibilities remain conditional.

## Primary research comparison

These sources support the prior-art boundaries above. Their author-reported outcomes have not been independently reproduced in this update.

- [ALMA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.07755) searches executable memory designs, including retrieval and updates. It limits broad claims that learning a memory design is new.
- [AIDE2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26457) recursively improves agent machinery with hidden evaluation. It limits broad recursive-improvement claims.
- [OPINE-World](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01531) revises unknown representations and programmatic world models together. Its ARC setting does not establish customer-operations performance.
- [Counterfactual Bootstrap for Robust Meta-Reinforcement Learning](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/bo26c.html) addresses confounded observational learning. Partial identification is established rather than a Lightwork invention.
- [Cordon](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17573) supplies task-scoped semantic transactions with staged effects, result lineage, authority, and recovery. Those execution primitives are not new claims here.
- [Negotiating Temporal Commitments in Cross-Organizational Business Processes](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.TIME.2020.4) is a direct temporal-commitment precedent for N01.
- [The Safety Filter](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-control-071723-102940) reviews safety-critical control in autonomous systems. It is a control and viability comparison for N01; business-specific applicability still needs to be established.
- [FAT-CG](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-35773-2) develops federated industrial-protocol test generation. N02 cannot claim privacy-preserving cross-organization executable testing generally as new.
- [Proof-Carrying Agent Actions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.04104) addresses runtime-neutral action evidence. N03 must add a genuine transport and qualification algorithm.
- [Formal skill containment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23951) is a comparison lead for qualified portable execution; no independent reproduction is claimed here.
- [Cross-organization process prediction transfer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12599-025-00969-9) is a process-transfer comparison for N03; prediction transfer alone does not establish executable procedure transport.
- [Coordination Avoidance in Database Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/1402.2237) establishes invariant-confluence reasoning. N04 must add to invariant-aware execution and batching.
- [Value of Information](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.06407) addresses human-agent communication through downstream utility and question cost. Asking economically useful questions is not new by itself.
- [Adaptive Submodularity](https://arxiv.org/abs/1003.3967) supplies established foundations for adaptive information gathering; N05 needs a distinct joint certificate algorithm rather than a new name for acquisition.
- [Discovering User-Interpretable Capabilities of Black-Box Planning Agents](https://aair-lab.github.io/genplan22/papers/verma_GenPlan22.pdf) learns capabilities using traces and reachability queries. N06 must add to capability learning and ordinary abduction under its declared information boundary.
